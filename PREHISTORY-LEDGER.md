# ADF PREHISTORY LEDGER

The complete record of ADF-related activity on this account **before the
machine existed**. Compiled from the account's full X data archive
(requested 2026-07-24, delivered 2026-07-25); finalized 2026-08-01.
Nothing gradable is omitted. Misses and promotional excess are preserved
alongside hits — a curated record would be worthless.

## Scope and method
- Source: the official X archive — 57 posts (2023-09-10 to 2026-05-09)
  plus 1 deleted post whose content the archive does not preserve.
- Classification: FORECAST (pre-registered, checkable), ANALYSIS
  (interpretive, no testable claim), PROMO (self-promotional), PERSONAL
  (unrelated to markets or ADF).
- Curation disclosure: in July 2026, 31 personal replies (politics,
  religion, misc.) were deleted from the account as personal-account
  cleanup before this professional relaunch. They contained no market
  content and were never part of any forecast record. Their existence
  and count are disclosed here so the census sums to the full archive;
  the complete original archive is retained offline.
- Grading: forecasts decompose into legs; each leg is stamped CONFIRM /
  DISCONFIRM / UNRESOLVED / UNGRADABLE against public outcomes.
  Consensus references from this era were informal; where a consensus
  is attested from contemporaneous reading rather than a frozen
  citation, this ledger says so plainly. (The machine that replaces
  this process freezes its consensus snapshot in git before the
  window opens — that discipline exists because of this gap.)
